# Visa and entry

Entry rules depend on the traveller's nationality and change often. Getting this wrong can
mean being refused boarding, so be careful and precise about what you know.

## Rules

1. **Ask for nationality (passport) if unknown**, and residence if it might matter. Without it,
   only give the general checklist below.
2. **Never state visa rules as definitive fact.** Guidebook directory sections describe the
   rules at publication time, which may be years out of date. Use them only for background
   and clearly label them as possibly outdated.
3. **Always point to the official source** for the final answer:
   - the destination country's official immigration or foreign-ministry site or embassy, and
   - the traveller's own government travel advice (e.g. travel.state.gov for US citizens,
     gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice for UK citizens, smartraveller.gov.au for Australians).
4. Recommend checking 2–4 weeks before departure and again just before flying.

## Checklist to walk through

- **Visa or travel authorisation:** whether one is needed for this passport and trip length,
  whether it must be obtained before travel (some countries use online pre-travel
  authorisations), processing time and cost.
- **Passport validity:** many countries require 6 months' validity beyond departure and blank
  pages; check the specific rule.
- **Onward/return ticket and proof of funds:** sometimes checked at the border.
- **Transit countries:** layovers can need their own visa or authorisation.
- **Health:** required vaccinations (e.g. yellow fever certificates when arriving from certain
  countries), recommended vaccines, medication import rules. Point to the user's doctor or a
  travel clinic and official health guidance.
- **Customs:** food, plant and cash declaration limits, banned items.
- **Travel insurance:** medical cover and evacuation, especially for remote or adventure
  travel; some countries require it.
- **Minors:** consent letters when travelling with one parent or without parents.

## Format

Give a short checklist tailored to the trip, mark each item as "check" rather than "required"
unless it's a universal basic, and finish with the official links to verify.
